The Problem

You're hitting MQL targets and missing the revenue number.

Sales and marketing don't talk. Different KPIs. Different timelines. Different strategies. It is not a culture problem — it is a structural one. Marketing has never had visibility into what is happening in deals. Sales has never had the context to understand what marketing is doing. The result: marketing builds strategy with half the data.

01

Marketing has never seen the bottom of the funnel

What buyers say in deals. What objections are killing close rates. Why you are really losing. That data exists — in call recordings, in closed-lost notes, in emails. Your marketing team has never had access to any of it.

02

The intelligence that does cross the wall is stale by the time it arrives

A rep mentions something in a QBR. A PMM hears it in a sales meeting six weeks later. By then the market has moved. The window has closed. The battlecard is wrong.

03

Content is built on assumptions because buyer data never makes it upstream

Your content team publishes what they think buyers want to read. The evidence of what buyers actually care about is in hundreds of call recordings nobody has synthesized.

04

Campaigns optimize for lead flow when they should optimize for revenue

That gap does not close on its own. It closes when marketing has the same picture sales has — and can build a strategy that reaches all the way to the close.

The Solution

The complete, updated picture to act on.

DARK MATTER performs time-series synthesis on the data siloed in sales tools, marketing analytics, customer intelligence, and competitor analysis to show what's happening in your funnel, why, and what to do next to accelerate growth.

C1

Customers

Voice-of-customer intelligence. Win/loss patterns, buyer language, ICP drift, churn signals. What buyers say when they are not in a sales conversation.

C2

Competitors

Hiring signals, funding moves, pricing shifts, positioning changes, review velocity. What competitors do — not what they say.

C3

Content

What buyers search for and find. Demand patterns, SERP ownership, AI citation gaps, which content actually moves deals versus which sits unused.

C4

Conversations

What is happening inside your deals. Call patterns, objection frequency, competitive mentions, rep behavior — synthesized at the portfolio level, not by individual rep.

C5

Chronology

The dimension that makes the other four compound. A battlecard is accurate right up until a competitor changes their price. Chronology tracks how all five signals move together over time — so the briefing you receive is a living picture, not a snapshot.

Most intelligence is a snapshot. DARK MATTER is a continuous picture. Every briefing is built on the same five categories, tracked over time, so patterns surface before they become problems.
